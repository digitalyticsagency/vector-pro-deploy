How much does a commercial fire alarm system cost?

It depends on the size of the building, the category of protection your fire risk assessment calls for, and whether a conventional or addressable system suits you best. After a free survey we give you a fixed written price, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

What does BAFE SP203-1 certification mean for me?

BAFE SP203-1 is the UK's independent scheme for companies that design, install, commission and maintain fire detection and alarm systems. It shows an auditor has checked our work, and it gives you a certificate that insurers and fire officers recognise.

Can you take over a system another company installed?

Yes. We take over and maintain conventional, addressable, wireless and bespoke systems from most manufacturers. We start with a health check so you know the condition of what you have.

How often does a fire alarm need servicing?

BS 5839-1 recommends servicing by a competent person at least every six months, with a weekly call point test carried out on site. We schedule the visits and keep the records for you.

Which areas do you cover?

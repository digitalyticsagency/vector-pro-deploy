Common questions

How much does a commercial fire alarm cost?

It depends on the building size, the category your fire risk assessment needs and whether conventional or addressable suits you. After the free survey you get one fixed written price before any work starts.

How often does a fire alarm need servicing?

BS 5839-1 recommends a service by a competent person at least every six months, plus a weekly call point test on site. We schedule the visits and keep the records for you.

Can you take over a system another company installed?

Yes. We take over conventional, addressable, wireless and bespoke systems from most manufacturers, starting with a health check.

Which areas do you cover?

All London boroughs, plus Maidstone, Chatham, Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells, Canterbury and the rest of Kent.

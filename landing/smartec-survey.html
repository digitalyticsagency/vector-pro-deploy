Good to know

Questions answered

Is fire alarm maintenance really a legal requirement?

Yes. The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 places a legal duty on building owners and managers to keep fire alarm systems regularly tested and maintained. Non-compliance can lead to fines and, in the most serious cases, imprisonment.

What does the £29.99 cover?

Scheduled mandatory fire alarm testing, the free digital logbook and compliance portal, inspection-ready records, priority response with 24/7 support, and no upfront upgrade costs. Your exact price depends on the size of your system and is confirmed at the free check.

An online record of every certificate, asset and test for your building. You can see your compliance status any time and share records with inspectors, insurers or landlords in seconds.

Yes. CCTV, access control and intruder alarms can be added to the same contract and the same portal.
